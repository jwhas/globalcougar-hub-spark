# Careers and employment

## Guidance

Use CareerLaunch, Handshake, and BYU Workday for career exploration and job
searching. Separate job-search help from work authorization: a job posting or
employer offer does not prove that a student is authorized to work.

For F-1/J-1 employment eligibility, on-campus work, CPT, OPT, internships,
unpaid roles, remote work, or Social Security Number steps, send the student
to ISSS before they begin work or training. Never state that a particular
arrangement is permitted without official individualized confirmation.

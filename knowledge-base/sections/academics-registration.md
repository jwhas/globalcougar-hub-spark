# Academics and registration

Direct registration, academic calendar, course load, tuition, and records
questions to the BYU Registrar, Financial Services, or the student's academic
advising office as appropriate.

If a student in F-1 or J-1 status is considering dropping a class or reducing
credits, refer them to ISSS before they act because enrollment changes can
affect immigration requirements.

For tutoring, accessibility, health, counseling, student life, and academic
support, route students to the responsible official office. Do not state
current deadlines or requirements without checking the current source.

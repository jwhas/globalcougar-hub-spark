# Financial resources and scholarships

Direct tuition, payment, billing, and student-account questions to BYU
Financial Services. Direct scholarship questions to BYU Scholarships and the
relevant college or department.

Clearly distinguish confirmed awards from examples. Scholarship names,
deadlines, eligibility, fees, and funding information must not be treated as
current without checking the official source. Do not request banking
credentials, payment-card details, or other confidential financial information.

## Official resources

- BYU Financial Services: <https://finserve.byu.edu/>
- BYU Scholarships: <https://enrollment.byu.edu/scholarships>

**Authority:** Official BYU. **Verification:** Check current opportunities,
requirements, and deadlines. Any scholarship shown in the prototype may be
demo content unless confirmed on the official scholarship page.

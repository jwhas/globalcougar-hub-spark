# BYU admissions

## Guidance

Direct prospective students to BYU Admissions and the specific international
applicant information. Admission requirements, application components, English
proficiency requirements, deadlines, and program requirements must be checked
on the current official page. Graduate applicants should use their specific
graduate program because requirements and deadlines may differ.

Do not estimate admission chances or promise that a document will be
accepted. Include a direct official source link for procedural or
time-sensitive information.

## Official resource

- BYU Admissions: <https://admissions.byu.edu/>
- BYU admissions main page: <https://www.byu.edu/admissions>

# Housing and campus life

## Guidance

Before arrival, students may need to plan housing check-in, local phone
service, transportation, banking, health screening or immunizations, and ISSS
check-in. These are planning suggestions, not universal BYU requirements.
Housing details depend on whether the student uses BYU-managed or
off-campus housing.

For health screening and immunizations, link the current BYU health
information and do not provide medical advice or assume identical
requirements. Bank requirements vary; do not guarantee an account or
recommend a specific product as universally suitable.
